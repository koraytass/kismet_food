Privacy Policy

Kismet Foods is committed to protecting the integrity of your data as diligently as we protect the quality of our global harvest.

01

Data Philosophy

As a global curator of fine ingredients, Kismet Foods ("we", "us", "our") understands that transparency is the foundation of trust. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our global trade platform and services.

We prioritize data minimization, ensuring that we only gather the information strictly necessary to facilitate global logistics, sustainability reporting, and premium client relations.

02

Information We Collect

badge

Identity & Business

Company name, tax identification, representative contact details, and trade credentials required for international shipping.

public

Logistics Metadata

IP addresses, port of entry preferences, and digital footprints left during the quote request process to optimize routing.

03

How We Use Information

  • check_circle Service Orchestration: To process high-volume bulk orders and coordinate with origin-specific logistics partners.
  • check_circle Compliance Monitoring: To ensure all trade movements align with international law and our own sustainability standards.
  • check_circle Market Intelligence: To provide you with personalized insights into origin harvest cycles and price fluctuations.

Global Security Standards

Our data centers are positioned globally to ensure low-latency access while maintaining localized sovereignty compliance, matching the regional expertise of our spice and bean sourcing teams.

04

Sharing & Transfers

Kismet Foods does not sell your personal or business data. Information is shared only with vetted third-party logistics (3PL) providers and customs agencies necessary to execute your trade orders.

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3) for all data transfers between our global offices and origin partners.
